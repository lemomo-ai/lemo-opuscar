# Pictogram Motion — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *Left Behind* 《被落下的伞》 (50.0 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps) · `pictogram-motion.mp4` · source in [`demo/`](demo/)

A yellow umbrella is left on a city bus on a rainy morning. All day in the sun nobody needs it; at the end of the line it joins 312 other lost umbrellas on the depot's shelf. At rush hour the storm comes back, the depot opens every shelf, and 313 umbrellas go back out into the rain. The hero is an object, not a person: the cast is an umbrella, a bus, doors, stop signs, a clock, clouds, sun, moon, lightning, a lost-property sign and footprints, all built from the same round-capped geometry as the grid pattern. English narration (Kokoro `bf_emma`) with Chinese and English subtitles. Line 4, the street names, the depot and the number 313 are fiction.

## Story & structure

Hook: rain, and on the first beat a yellow umbrella snaps open (the only warm colour in a blue world) over a trail of wet footprints. Then the sun comes out, dry footprints step off the bus, the umbrella stays on its rail. Turn: half a beat of silence, thunder, and the umbrella that nobody needed in the sun becomes what the whole city needs. Landing: the shelf of 313 folded umbrellas opens at once and turns into the film's own pattern; the next morning at 07:42 it rains again and the footprints under the yellow umbrella are dry, answering the first shot.

Candidate structures considered: a chain of shape changes (one disc becomes sun, wheel, clock, coin; no subject, no turn) and a countdown of ten city moments (a list again). One object's day won: it needs objects, vehicles, weather, signs and actions for real reasons, and the signature (the pattern) grows from one canopy to a shelf to the whole frame.

Native moves spent: *one verb per item* (the umbrella opens, furls, turns over, hooks on, drips), *chapter = colour* (navy rain, teal day, coral depot, ink storm, yellow bloom), *long take down a track* (the line, one stop per beat), *pace ladder* (one-second clock cards), *count-up slam* (313), *icon wall* (the shelf turns yellow), *split screen along a grid line* (wet stop / dry shelf), and one of our own: *the wall becomes the pattern* (313 canopies open into half-discs and the rows shift like the core graphic).

## Score structure

120 BPM, picture-locked: `mix.py` places every hit from the page's `window.EV` (exported to `events.json`), so cuts, slams, steps and stops land on their frames.

- Instruments: a synthesised electronic kick under everything, sampled marimba (rain drips, ostinati, the count run), glockenspiel (bells, the sun going down, one note per stop), bowed vibraphone pads, electric bass, shaker (rain), hi-hat, claps, log drum and claves (the depot), frame drum (the storm), crash.
- Key follows the weather: D minor in the rain → F major when the sun comes out (a glockenspiel run on the flip) → D minor at the depot and in the storm → the film's first F♯ when the sign turns to TAKE ONE → D major for the bloom and the next morning.
- Silences: half a beat before the title (6.75–7.0), before the depot (18.25–18.5) and before the bloom (36.25–36.5); 26–28 a single ticking clock, then thunder as the first sound of the storm.
- Foley by event: canopy snap, wet and dry steps, furl, rail clink, air brakes, stop bell, tile clicks, door hiss, whips, drips, shutter clanks, a ripple of 14 canopy pops on the bloom, a pop per stop. Rain beds: bright outside, muffled while the camera is inside the bus, heavy in the storm.
- Voice compressed, then about 10 dB over the music by RMS; music ducks and dips 300 Hz–4 kHz under each line.
- Measured on the master: −14.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −4.0 dBTP.

## End card

Navy pattern whose rows stop one by one, the yellow umbrella opening on its stage disc, and the type sliding up from 46.5 s, fully in place from about 47.5 s to 50.0 s: LEFT BEHIND / 被落下的伞, a hairline, PICTOGRAM MOTION 象形运动图形, and "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5". The last narration line runs under it at the bottom until 49.05 s. The sign-off belongs to this library's demo only; a user's film carries no LemoLab credit and no copy of this card.

## Build

`sh styles/pictogram-motion/demo/build.sh` (`WORKERS=3` for more render workers, `VOICE=1` to regenerate the narration, `sh demo/build.sh 28 36` for a span).

- Needs the voice tier and three sample libraries: `sh tools/setup.sh deps voice`, `sh tools/fetch.sh instruments vcsl`, `… vsco2ce`, `… freepats`. The three fonts are fetched into `demo/fonts/` on the first run (`tools/fetch_fonts.sh`).
- Steps: narration (`voice/lines.json` → Kokoro → `out/voice/`, `asr_check.py`), captions (`tools/cues.py`: line starts in `voice/vo.json` + clip lengths → `voice/cues.js` and `../pictogram-motion.srt`; it refuses a caption shorter than max(1.8 s, line + 0.6 s)), events, picture, `mix.py`, `mux.sh`, readcheck, poster and the gallery style frame (t = 9.2, rendered with `?nocap=1`).
- The page takes `?a=&b=` (render a span of the film; `render(t)` always draws film second a + t) and `?nocap=1`.
- `render(t)` keeps no state between frames. About 45 s for the whole film with two workers on an M-series Mac.
- `readcheck.mjs` passes everything except two known kinds: the three pace-ladder cards (13.5–16.5, one second each by design, the narration says the same thing) and the TAKE ONE sign (1.5 s; it reads as a pictogram and the line "The depot opened every shelf" plays over it).

## Engine reference

### `engine.js` (window.G)

| API | What it does |
|---|---|
| `G.E.{lin,io,o3,i3,io3,oExp,iExp,ioExp,oBack,o5}` | easings (input clamped 0..1) |
| `G.clamp, G.lerp, G.inv(a,b,x)`, `G.rng(seed)`, `G.hash(n)` | maths; `inv` = progress of x between a and b, used for every timing |
| `G.PAL[key]` (`t`, `fg`, `far`), `G.CREAM`, `G.INK`, `G.UMB`, `G.mix(a,b,t)`, `G.rgba(hex,a)` | palette and colour helpers |
| `G.pattern(pk, seed, {spread, density, set})` | cached pattern canvas, 3 cells wider than the screen; `set` = `'rain'` or `'sun'` motif library |
| `G.drawPattern(ctx, pat, offs(row)→px)` | draws the pattern with a per-row horizontal offset (wraps, so rows can travel) |
| `G.motif(ctx, kind, x, y, size, [bg, main, sub], rng)` | one patterned cell (kinds 0–13 geometry, 14–20 pictogram cells) |
| `G.umbrella(ctx, x, y, R, open, rot, canopy, gore, shaft)` | umbrella; `open` 0 furled → 1 open; origin = canopy rim centre |
| `G.drop`, `G.cloud`, `G.sunIcon`, `G.moon`, `G.bolt`, `G.foot(…, mirror)`, `G.item(ctx, kind, …)` | weather, footprint and lost-item pictograms |
| `G.disc`, `G.seg`, `G.ring`, `G.poly`, `G.rrect`, `G.hair` | round-capped drawing primitives |
| `G.maskText(ctx, text, x, y, font, col, p, {align, ls})`, `G.typeText(…)` | mask slide-up entrance; typewriter |

### `film.js`

`SHOTS` (film seconds and a `kind` per shot), `TRANS` (grid transition per shot boundary: `rows`, `cols`, `slide`, `split`, `quarter`, `flip`), `EV` (sound events). One function per kind (`rainOpen`, `busStop`, `titleCard`, `track`, `ladder`, `lastStop`, `lost`, `wall`, `storm`, `split`, `shutter`, `bloom`, `route`, `echo`, `endCard`); shared pieces `textBlock`, `chip`, `stopSign`, `drawBus`, `drawWall` (the shelf, with `open`, `sweep` and `shift` callbacks), `hud`, `subtitles`. `frame(t)` handles the boundary transitions and the custom ones (flip, iris, descent, lightning split); `render` averages five sub-frames inside the `FAST` windows.

### Minimal example: a new pictogram and a card

```js
// engine.js: a kite, built from the same geometry
G.kite = (ctx, x, y, s, col, line) => {
  G.poly(ctx, [[x, y - s], [x + s * 0.6, y], [x, y + s], [x - s * 0.6, y]], col);
  G.seg(ctx, [x, y + s], [x - s * 0.3, y + s * 2.2], s * 0.06, line);
};
// film.js: a card that uses it, entered with the shared text block
function kiteCard(ctx, t) {
  const P = PAL.sky, lt = t - 50;
  ctx.fillStyle = P.t[2]; ctx.fillRect(0, 0, W, H);
  G.drawPattern(ctx, G.pattern('sky', 7, { spread: 0.42, set: 'sun' }), rowDrift(lt, 1));
  stageDisc(ctx, 1290, 520, 360, P.t[4], lt);
  G.kite(ctx, 1290, 420 + Math.sin(lt * Math.PI) * 20, 140, G.UMB, P.fg);   // tugs on the beat
  textBlock(ctx, lt, 130, 540, { title: 'WIND', px: 230, zh: '起风了', zpx: 70, fg: P.fg });
}
```
Then add `{ t0, kind: 'kite' }` to `SHOTS`, a case in `drawShot`, a transition in `TRANS` and its sound in `EV`.

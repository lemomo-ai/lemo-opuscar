# 「买你这一下」 What Your Tap Costs: facts

Every number and claim in the voice-over and on screen, its source and the wording it rests on. Each is marked as one of:

- **Company disclosure**: the company's own prospectus, annual report or results announcement (directly, or quoted by the press: "via").
- **Third-party estimate**: a data firm, broker or analyst (DataEye, 伽马数据, 券商研报, 运营研究社). The film labels these on screen the same way.
- **Single case**: one anonymous product's numbers from an industry article; the film calls it 某款传奇 ("a legend game").
- **Mechanism / illustration**: how the business works, with no figure, or a figure drawn only to show the shape (the film labels it 示意).

Company names on screen are partly masked (贪▒游戏, 恺▒网络《蓝▒传奇》). No company is named in the voice; no star's face, name or voice is used.

# FACTS · 《安检口的夜班》 Night Shift at Lane 3

Every rule shown on screen, its source and the quoted wording. Fetched 2026-10-24.
The lane is an **international departures** lane: the 100 mL rule quoted below is the one for international and regional flights (on domestic flights in China liquids may not be carried in the cabin at all, except toiletries in containers of 100 mL or less).

## 1. Power banks: 100 Wh / 160 Wh

On screen: label photo `20000mAh 3.7V 74Wh`; formula `20000 mAh ÷ 1000 × 3.7 V = 74 Wh`; gauge zones `≤100 Wh 随身带` (cabin bag), `100–160 Wh 航司批准 · 每人限 2 块` (airline approval · max 2 per person), `>160 Wh 禁止` (banned); verdict `可以随身带 · ≤ 100 Wh · CABIN BAG, NO APPROVAL`.

- Source: CAAC, 《关于民航旅客携带"充电宝"乘机规定的公告》 (Notice on the rules for air passengers carrying power banks), 2014-08-07, listed as 有效 (in force).
  https://www.caac.gov.cn/XXGK/XXGK/TZTG/201511/t20151105_11173.html
  > 二、充电宝只能在手提行李中携带或随身携带，严禁在托运行李中携带。 [2. Power banks may only be carried in hand luggage or on the person; carrying them in checked baggage is strictly prohibited.]
  > 三、充电宝额定能量不超过100Wh,无需航空公司批准；额定能量超过100Wh但不超过160Wh，经航空公司批准后方可携带，但每名旅客不得携带超过两个充电宝。 [3. Power banks with a rated energy of no more than 100 Wh need no airline approval; those above 100 Wh but no more than 160 Wh may be carried only with airline approval, and no passenger may carry more than two power banks.]
  > 四、严禁携带额定能量超过160Wh的充电宝；…… [4. Power banks with a rated energy above 160 Wh are strictly prohibited; …]
  > 附：充电宝额定能量的判定方法 …… Wh= V x Ah …… 如果充电宝上只标记有毫安时(mAh)，可将该数值除以1000得到安培小时(Ah)。 [Appendix: how to determine a power bank's rated energy … Wh = V x Ah … If a power bank is marked only in milliampere-hours (mAh), divide the value by 1000 to get ampere-hours (Ah).]
- Same rule restated: CAAC 《航空旅行常识》 (Air travel basics), section 行李运输 (baggage), 2023-03-16.
  https://www.caac.gov.cn/INDEX/HLFW/HKLXCS/202303/t20230316_217597.html
  > 便携式电子设备中的锂电池、备用锂电池（含充电宝）的额定能量不超过100Wh时，可以随身携带，但禁止作为行李托运；如果大于100Wh，但不超过160Wh（含）时，需经航空公司批准后，方可运输，仅可随身携带且每人限带2块。 [When the rated energy of lithium batteries in portable electronic devices and spare lithium batteries (including power banks) is no more than 100 Wh, they may be carried on the person but not checked in as baggage; above 100 Wh but no more than 160 Wh (inclusive), they may be transported only with airline approval, only as carry-on, and at most 2 per person.]
- Check: 20000 mAh ÷ 1000 = 20 Ah; 20 Ah × 3.7 V = 74 Wh ≤ 100 Wh. The label values are invented for a generic, unbranded power bank.
- Not shown: since 2025-06-28 power banks without a CCC (3C) mark or from recalled batches may not be carried on **domestic** flights (CAAC news, https://www.caac.gov.cn/XWZX/MHYW/202506/t20250626_227805.html). Our lane is international, so the film does not use it.

## 2. Liquids: 100 mL per container (international flights)

On screen: measurement `21 cm`, `Ø 6.5 cm`, `容器约 500 mL` (container about 500 mL); rule `国际航班：单个容器 ≤ 100 mL / INTERNATIONAL: EACH CONTAINER ≤ 100 mL`; a 100 mL container outline drawn to scale; verdict `不能随身带 · OVER 100 mL · NOT IN CABIN BAG`; the bottle is taken out and the bag rescanned.

- Source: CAAC 《航空旅行常识》 (Air travel basics), 2023-03-16 (URL above).
  > 2.旅客乘坐国际、地区航班时，液态物品应当盛放在单体容器容积不超过100mL的容器内随身携带，且盛放液态物品的容器应置于最大容积不超过1L、可重新封口的透明塑料袋中，每名旅客每次仅允许携带一个透明塑料袋，超出部分应作为行李托运。 [2. On international and regional flights, liquids carried on the person must be in containers of no more than 100 mL each, placed in a resealable clear plastic bag of no more than 1 L; each passenger may carry only one clear plastic bag per trip, and anything beyond that must be checked in as baggage.]
  > 注意事项 1.……指的是盛放化妆品的容器的大小不得超过100毫升，而不是指容器内的物质的多少。 [Notes 1. … means that the container holding the cosmetics may not be larger than 100 mL, not how much is inside the container.]
- Original notice: 《中国民航总局关于限制携带液态物品乘坐民航飞机的公告》 (CAAC notice on restricting liquids carried on civil aircraft), 2007-03-17, listed as 有效 (in force).
  http://www.caac.gov.cn/XXGK/XXGK/ZFGW/201601/t20160122_27549.html
  > 二、乘坐从中国境内机场始发的国际、地区航班的旅客，其携带的液态物品每件容积不得超过100毫升（ml）。 [2. Passengers on international and regional flights departing from airports in China may carry liquids only in containers of no more than 100 millilitres (ml) each.]
- Domestic flights (why the lane is international): 《关于禁止旅客随身携带液态物品乘坐国内航班的公告》 (Notice prohibiting passengers from carrying liquids on domestic flights), 2008-03-14, http://www.caac.gov.cn/XWZX/MHYW/200803/t20080318_11842.html
  > 一、乘坐国内航班的旅客一律禁止随身携带液态物品，但可办理交运…… [1. Passengers on domestic flights may not carry liquids on their person at all, but may check them in …]
- The verdict concerns the container (500 mL), which is what the rule measures. 500 mL is the bottle's nominal size; the screen reads it as an estimate (`约` "about", `≈`) from the measured size.

## 3. Live hairy crabs in a cabin bag

On screen: `活蟹 × 6 · 已捆扎 / LIVE CRABS × 6 · TIED`; rule `活体海鲜不能随身带进客舱 / LIVE SEAFOOD MAY NOT GO IN CABIN BAGS`; verdict `不能带进客舱 / NOT ALLOWED IN THE CABIN`.

- 中国民航网 (CAAC's news site, published by 中国民航报社, the CAAC newspaper), 《白云安检提醒您：大闸蟹不能随身携带登机》 (Baiyun airport security reminds you: hairy crabs may not be carried on board), 2019-10-09.
  http://caacnews.com.cn/1/5/201910/t20191009_1282548.html
  > 1.海鲜等活体动物不能作为旅客的随身行李带进客舱，必须办理行李托运手续。 [1. Seafood and other live animals may not be carried into the cabin as passengers' carry-on baggage; they must be checked in.]
  > 另外，各航空公司对海鲜的运输规定标准不一，具体规定请广大旅客朋友在出行前与航空公司联系。 [Also, airlines' rules for carrying seafood differ; passengers should contact their airline for the specific rules before travelling.]
- China Eastern (Wuhan), quoted by 极目新闻 (Jimu News), 2022-11-08: https://www.ctdsb.net/c1666_202211/1560563.html
  > 大闸蟹和海鲜等属于鲜活易腐物品，易污染飞机货舱，所以禁止作为随身行李携带上机。大闸蟹可以进行托运…… [Hairy crabs and seafood are fresh, live and perishable goods that easily contaminate the aircraft hold, so they are prohibited as carry-on baggage. Hairy crabs can be checked in …]
- Airline rules differ only on **checked** transport (some airlines take crabs as checked baggage, some don't; 中国经济网 (China Economic Net) 2017 lists airlines without ventilated holds). None of the sources allows them in the cabin, and an international destination may refuse live crabs altogether, so the film says only "not in the cabin" and does not tell the passenger to check them in.

## 4. Dual-energy colours

On screen legend: 有机物 organic Zeff < 10 orange; 混合物 mixed / inorganic Zeff 10–18 green; 金属 metal Zeff > 18 blue; 穿不透 impenetrable black. This is the common convention of dual-energy baggage X-ray (orange organic, green mixed/light inorganic, blue metal, black where nothing gets through); the Zeff bands are the usual approximate ranges, shown as a generic legend, not a specific manufacturer's.

## Invented for the film

The lane, the times, the counter (1,284 bags, 37 opened, 412 power banks, 29 liquids, 6 crabs), the bag contents and the console are fictional. No real company, product or logo appears.

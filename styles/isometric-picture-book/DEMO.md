# Isometric People Scene — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *Seventy-Two Seats* 《七十二个座位》 (59.8 s, Chinese narration, bilingual subtitles) · `isometric-picture-book.mp4` · `isometric-picture-book.srt` · source in [`demo/`](demo/)

## Story & structure

Boarding a plane always takes ages, even when rows are called in order. The film shows why (one aisle: one person stopping to stow a bag stops everyone behind, and everyone called is heading for the same few rows), then a different order (window seats first, each person one row apart from the next in line) in which six people stow bags at once, and the cabin fills in nearly half the time.

The two orders and both times come from a real test: Steffen & Hotchkiss (2012), a 12-row, six-abreast mock cabin with one 21-inch aisle and 72 passengers; back rows first in blocks took 6:54, window first and every other row 3:36 (sources and quotes in [`demo/FACTS.md`](demo/FACTS.md)). Gate B12, the aircraft and its livery are fictional; no airline appears.

Arc: the question (why so long?) → the usual answer (back rows first) → why it jams (one aisle; one person stops, all wait; everyone heads for the same stretch) → 6:54 → what if the order changes? → window first, one row apart → six at once → 3:36 → the two times side by side → the real test and its 72 passengers → the answer: not crowds, one stretch of aisle → sign-off.

Native moves spent: floor type (title, key words, numbers, sign-off); domino stand-up; lid off (twice); order lighting; order arrows; weave fill; bars on the ground; people as the sample; the jammed stretch lit as "stand here".

- Chinese (the library film): edge-tts `zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural`, rate +25 %. The line lengths are written into `VO_DUR` in `film.js`, which builds the 14 shots from them.
- English (`build.sh en`): edge-tts `en-US-AvaNeural`, rate +10 % (V02 and V08 at +25 %, V06 at +20 % to fit their shots), each line starting with its Chinese line; same picture, score and subtitles.
- Spoken-only spellings (`say`, the subtitle text is unchanged): V05 has short pauses so 相邻 and 全挤 are clear; V12 reads the year as 二零一二年 (English: "twenty twelve"); English V02 has two short pauses. Speech check: `asr_check.py` with the medium Whisper models; `asr` fields give Whisper's spelling where it writes digits (6分54秒, 3分36秒, 2012, 72) and where it hears 全挤 as the near-homophone 拳击.

## Score structure

100 BPM, F major, under the voice. Opening F → B♭; the gate a pizzicato walk under the line; a harp sweep as the lid opens; a stuck pizzicato stab and a held cello D while the line waits; piano sixteenths for the time-lapse and a low Dm on 6:54; one second of score backwards for the rewind and a glockenspiel run as the seats light; a rising vibraphone F A C F at the second gate; one six-note vibraphone chord per wave in the weave with pizzicato and shaker under it; F with glockenspiel on 3:36; B♭ → Gm under the bars and C on the bracket; F/A under the 72 passengers, Dm under the callback, a long F under the sign-off. A soft cello root sustains under every shot and overlaps each cut. The music ducks 16 dB under the narration and lifts between lines. Mix: −16.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −2.1 dBFS before mux (English: −16.0 / −2.3).

## Palette & props

Palette as in STYLE.md §3 (paper, concrete, carpet, ink, persimmon accent, subject green, ginger markings). Props: a fictional rear-engined regional jet with a T-tail, white with two green bands; 12 rows of 3 + 3 green seats with cream headrest covers and low backs; overhead bins on the cut rims, one per row on the near side; a 7.5 m jet bridge with a glazed far wall and a low near wall; a gate with three painted lanes, group signs, a podium and three rows of pale green chairs; a baggage train on the service road; two time bars and a cabin-shaped formation of 72 people on the apron. People: 72, four tops, each with a rolling case.

## End card

On the stand, "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5" lands word by word in the floor-type serif and holds 3 s, fully inside the frame. Under it, in the small floor-type serif, the licence credit for the Salamander piano samples (CC BY 3.0) fades in as the shot opens, so it holds to the end. The "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5" sign-off belongs to this library's demo only; a user's film carries no LemoLab credit and no copy of this card.

## Build notes

- `sh styles/isometric-picture-book/demo/build.sh` (Chinese, `isometric-picture-book.mp4`) or `build.sh en` (`isometric-picture-book-en.mp4`, reusing the rendered picture): fonts (downloaded from google/fonts, Noto Serif SC subset by `tools/subset.py`) → sample packs → narration (edge-tts, online) + speech check → `tools/vo_lengths.py` (the Chinese voice must still match `VO_DUR`) → events → `audio.py` (narration, score, foley, ducking, loudness) → picture → master → readcheck → `tools/srt.py` (bilingual .srt) → poster and gallery still.
- Files: `engine.js`, `kit.js` (style kit, two-line subtitles), `plane.js`, `places.js`, `film.js` (timeline from the narration, queue simulation, 14 shot functions, `texts()`, `events()`), `main.js` / `index.html`, `audio.py`, `lines.json` / `lines_en.json`, `FACTS.md`, `tools/`.
- Checks: readcheck passes on all world text (the subtitle cues follow the narration and go to the .srt); `tools/motion_check.mjs` reports no jumps, no stop-start stepping and no two standing people closer than 0.35 m. Render ≈ 30–60 ms per 1080p frame.
- Demo-prop notes: the terminal glass is cut to a parapet in every shot that looks past it; type beside the fuselage stays between the jet bridge and the wing (single line, scaled down where needed); the verdict bars sit far enough ahead of the nose that the nose never covers the cabin icons; the gate's seat numbers and group signs show only in their own shots.

Clawd

Clawd, the small orange character, is Anthropic's: its design and its name
belong to Anthropic. The files that REUSE.toml marks with LicenseRef-Clawd are
pictures in which Clawd appears, and the code that draws it: pictures from
OPUSCAR 98, from the thank-you films for 300, 800, 3,000 and 6,000 stars and
from the demo film of Mascot Screencast, and that demo's drawing code.

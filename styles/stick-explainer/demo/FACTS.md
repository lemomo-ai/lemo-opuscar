# Facts in «Six Hands Away»

Every number on screen and in the voice-over, with its source. Illustrative material is labelled as such on screen.

| Claim in the film | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| "Just six handshakes, they say." | The popular "six degrees of separation" idea: Frigyes Karinthy, «Chains» (1929); John Guare, *Six Degrees of Separation* (play, 1990). | Presented as a saying, not a measurement. |
| 1967, Stanley Milgram tested it; letters passed only between people who knew each other | S. Milgram, "The Small World Problem", *Psychology Today* 1(1), 61–67 (1967); J. Travers & S. Milgram, "An Experimental Study of the Small World Problem", *Sociometry* 32(4), 425–443 (1969). | Starters were asked to send the folder only to a personal acquaintance (first-name basis) closer to the target. |
| 296 letters, 64 arrived | Travers & Milgram 1969: 296 starting persons, 64 chains reached the target. | Shown as "64 / 296 letters arrived". |
| 5.2 people in between, on average | Travers & Milgram 1969: mean number of intermediaries in completed chains 5.2. | The 1967 row in S4 shows "≈6 steps" (5.2 intermediaries ≈ 6.2 steps). |
| From Nebraska, to Boston | Travers & Milgram 1969: starting groups in Nebraska and in the Boston area; the target was a stockbroker working in Boston and living in Sharon, Massachusetts. The 1967 article also describes a starting group in Wichita, Kansas. | The film names only the Nebraska start. |
| Four chains, three of them break | Illustration of the low completion rate (64 of 296, about 22 %). | Not data. |
| 100 → 10,000 → … → 10,000,000,000 | Arithmetic: 100 acquaintances per person, 100^k at step k. | Labelled "illustrative · ignores overlap" on screen. |
| Earth 8,200,000,000 | United Nations, *World Population Prospects 2024*: world population about 8.2 billion in mid-2024. | |
| 4.74 steps (2011) | L. Backstrom, P. Boldi, M. Rosa, J. Ugander, S. Vigna, "Four Degrees of Separation", ACM Web Science 2012: average distance 4.74 among 721 million active Facebook users (2011). | Label "Facebook · 721 million people". |
| 3.57 steps (2016) | S. Bhagat, M. Burke, C. Diuk, I. O. Filiz, S. Edunov, "Three and a half degrees of separation", Facebook Research, 4 February 2016: average 3.57 degrees among about 1.6 billion people. | Label "Facebook · 1.6 billion people". |

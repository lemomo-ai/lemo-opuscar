# Neon Tubes — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *Midnight Snack* 《夜宵》 (48.0 s) · `neon-tubes.mp4` · source in [`demo/`](demo/)

A rainy night street of neon signs, told without a word. A turquoise neon cat wakes on the sign of an ice café, sees the goldfish on the sign of a goldfish shop at the end of the street, and goes for it, lighting every sign it lands on. Its pounce cracks the fishbowl: the water drains a level at a time and the fish fades. The cat takes the fish in its mouth, runs it home through the rain and drops it into the goblet of iced water on its own sign, where the fish strikes back to life. At closing time the street goes dark one sign at a time until only the goblet, the fish and the sleeping cat are left in a puddle. The shop names, street and times are invented; what is real is the medium, sourced in [`demo/FACTS.md`](demo/FACTS.md).

## Story & structure

Logline: a neon cat goes out for a midnight snack, breaks the goldfish's bowl, and carries the fish home to save it.

Arc: waking (an electrode fires in the cat's eye, light runs through its outline, its home sign strikes after it) → want (a whip pan down the dark street to the goldfish sign striking on the downbeat; a match cut from the round bowl to the cat's round eye) → the journey (each sign strikes as the cat lands: a clock it swats five hours forward, which brings the rain; a hanging hotel box; an arrow chaser that carries it like a conveyor; the karaoke board) → the mistake (two paws into the bowl, a wiggle, a pounce; the crack, three water lines going out one level at a time, the fish darker every beat; ears back, half a step back, a look, one silent beat) → the run home (chorus: down to the street, along the base of the wall with its twin in the puddles, over the alley, a wide where the street flashes after it, up the café's facade) → the goblet (the fish goes in and goes out, then strikes back on the heaviest beat of the film) → closing time (signs off one per beat, the cat curls up and its eye goes out, the last sign in a still puddle) → the end card, with one eye striking open at the fish.

Native moves spent: *strike* (eye, home sign, every landing, the revival, the last glance), *frame-switched character* (39 cat poses, 9 fish frames), *strike in sequence* (the journey), *chaser* (the arrow conveyor, bubbles), *alternate circuits* (the fish's tail, the microphone, window frames), *crack and drain* (the bowl), *crane to puddle level* and *puddle-level chase* (the run home), *rise from the reflection* (the climb), *one sign left* (the ending).

## Score structure

Electronic city pop in F major, 120 BPM, bar lines on even seconds; everything synthesised (`audio.py`).

| Section | Time | Palette | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wake | 0–4 | transformer hum, the cat's purr (hum with a 25 Hz tremor), filtered pad; kick from 2.0 | the eye's strike crackles; a bell when the light reaches the tail |
| Fish | 4–8 | bell motif A–C–D–C–A–G–F, 8th hats, bass pedal | the motif comes back softly at 16.0 |
| Verse | 8–15.5 | four-on-the-floor, claps on 2 and 4, 16th hats, syncopated bass, FM electric piano; B♭–C–Am–Dm | every sign that strikes plays the next note of a rising F pentatonic pluck line; ticks and a sweep while the clock spins |

Mix: the music sits under the foley and ducks by the foley's level; 3rd-order high-pass at 48 Hz; the pre-master is −16 LUFS with true peak −5.5 dBTP so that `mux.sh` lands at −14.0 LUFS, true peak −1.7 dB. All cue times come from `events.json`.

## Palette & props

- Cat: phosphor-coated turquoise outline, pink eye (almond, slit, wide or closed), whiskers and tail as thin tubes; sitting height about 0.95 m. Goldfish: clear tube with neon, red-orange, about 0.35 m.
- 金鱼 GOLDFISH: navy board #0a1626; a white phosphor fishbowl (R 0.5 m) breaking the board's top edge; three argon-blue water lines; white bubbles, yellow pebbles, green weed; 金鱼 in yellow phosphor, GOLDFISH in argon blue; aquarium tanks glowing in the shop window.
- 冰室 ICE CAFE: oxblood board #2a0b0d; an argon-blue goblet drawn with the same wavy water line as the fishbowl, two white ice cubes, a pink straw; 冰室 in neon, ICE CAFE in argon blue; a neon clock with an argon ring.
- 旅馆 HOTEL: a black box hanging 2 m in front of the wall (yellow 旅馆, pink HOTEL) and a neon arrow over the door with a head at each end. 歌厅 KARAOKE: aubergine board, a microphone in two circuits (pink / argon), window frames in pink and turquoise. 药房 PHARMACY: green crosses, white hanzi. A dark alley between the karaoke building and the goldfish shop.
- Facades: plaster, mosaic tile and brick; shopfronts with warm, cool and violet interiors; roller shutters after closing; AC units, drainpipes, cables.

## End card

The ice café's roller shutter, lit from above by the goblet: 夜宵 (Noto Sans SC 900), MIDNIGHT SNACK, 霓虹灯管 NEON TUBES, `LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5` (Barlow Condensed Bold, about 32 px), stencilled in the lower left of the shutter, away from the sign. 4.0 s, static; the eye-open gag plays on the sign above, never over the lines. The "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5" sign-off belongs to this library's demo only; a user's film carries no LemoLab credit and no copy of this card.

## Build notes

```
demo/
  build.sh       one command: fonts (if missing) → glyphs.json → art.json → events → mix → picture → master → readcheck → poster, styleframe
  index.html     one canvas; loads film.js
  film.js        render(t): timeline T, circuits (strike, chasers, the cracked bowl), character keys (K_, jump, run), fish keys,
                 cameras per shot, drawing (tubes, facade, clock, rain), uniforms for gl.js, TEXTS, EV
  world.js       gas colours, the street, signs, fishbowl and goblet placement, artTube
  lib.js         polylines, fillets, tubeFromStrokes (caps per end), hanzi / latin from glyphs.json
  gl.js          WebGL2: glass shading, surface lighting from the emission pyramid, wet street, puddles, ripples, post
  glyphs.json    neon hanzi and Latin capitals (tube centrelines)
  art.json       39 cat poses, 9 fish frames, the fishbowl and the goblet (generated)
  audio.py       score + foley from events.json -> mix.wav
  tools/glyphs_build.py   the neon hanzi, laid on Noto Sans SC's skeleton -> glyphs.json
  tools/glyph_check.py    overlay of every neon hanzi on the font, to check stroke positions
  tools/art.py            the pose rig and every pose (add a png path to get a sheet of all poses) -> art.json
  tools/sdf.py            signed-distance and polyline helpers for art.py
  tools/fonts.py          Noto Sans SC subset for the end card
  FACTS.md       the medium's facts, sources, quoted wording
```

- `sh demo/build.sh` (renders at 30 fps with `--workers 2`; `WORKERS=3` for more). Needs only the core tier: no voice model, no sample libraries. On the first run it downloads Barlow Condensed Bold and the Noto Sans SC variable font from github.com/google/fonts into `demo/fonts/` (gitignored). Delete `demo/fonts/NotoSansSC-sub.ttf` after changing the end card's text.
- 1080p: about 40 ms a frame on Apple silicon; the whole film renders in about 25 s with 3 workers. `render(t)` depends on `t` only: rain, ripples, flicker, poses and the crack are closed-form.
- Needs WebGL2 with `EXT_color_buffer_float` (falls back to 8-bit targets: flatter highlights).
- Readcheck (`node core/render/readcheck.mjs styles/neon-tubes/demo`) passes: 金鱼 / GOLDFISH 2.60 s (needs 2.03 s), 冰室 / ICE CAFE 4.48 s, the end card's four lines 3.96 s (the longest needs 2.99 s). The other signs are scenery and are not reported.
- Demo-specific pitfalls: in a macro the eye height must equal the subject's height, or the 5 cm stand-off of the tubes throws the subject out of frame; the match cut computes the eye's camera from the bowl's screen position and radius; a puddle-level reflection needs the mirrored object within two screen heights above the frame (the final tilt uses K = 280 for that); the cat's afterglow is limited to jumps, because on a run every frame's ghost reads as a second cat.

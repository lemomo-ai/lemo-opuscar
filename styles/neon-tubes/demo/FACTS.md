# Neon Tubes demo · sources for the medium

The street, the shop names and the clock times are invented. What is real is how neon signs are made and what colour each
gas gives; each line below matches something on screen.

| 2 | Clear tubes with argon and a little mercury glow blue (the goblet, the water lines, the clock ring, ICE CAFE, GOLDFISH) | same as 1 | "a small amount of mercury is added to the argon to produce an intense blue light." |

Shown as stylisation, not as fact: the argon-mercury warm-up compressed to about a second (a real tube takes longer to reach
full brightness); a crack that turns the glow pale violet and makes it retreat toward the electrodes; and the cat and the
goldfish moving by switching one tube per pose, after the sequenced frames of animated signs.

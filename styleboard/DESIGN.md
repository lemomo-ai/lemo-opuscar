# The Workbench (电影工坊; called 造物台 until 11-10): the site's design rules

The rules for every page of the Lemo-Opuscar site: the home page (a long take along a workbench), the style pages, OPUSCAR 98, the thank-you films and the 404 page, in English and in Chinese. The picture: **an old factory converted into a studio, at night. The words are paper under the lamps; the page is a thin layer of captions floating in the dark parts of the picture.**

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `assets/app.css` | the colour, type and size variables and the home page's components; every page loads it |
| `assets/page.css`, `page.js` | the pages under the bench (style pages, OPUSCAR 98, thank-you films, 404) |
| `assets/player.css`, `player.js` | the player, one component for every film on the site |
| `assets/app.js`, `engines.js` | the home page: scrolling scrubs the long take; `o98.js`: OPUSCAR 98's page |
| `home.py` | the home page's markup and words, the font subsets |
| `pages.py` | every other page, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, the search index; `build_site()` puts the site together |
| `sitedata.py` | the library as the site sees it (style.json, `i18n.json`, `gallery.py`) |
| `media.py` | posters, stills, sprites, OPUSCAR 98's frames; the label and top-bar measures |
| `site_config.py` | what a fork changes (repository, site address, where the films load from: the style films from the films repository, the others from `films/`) and this machine's folders |
| `bench.json`, `anchors.json` | which styles stand on the bench at each station; the old site's anchors (shared with the README) |
| `serve.py`, `check_site.py`, `labelcheck.mjs` | the local preview, the checks before publishing, where each label's lines sit |

The long take itself (frames, the stations' stop frames, the cards' thumbnails and clips, `manifest.json`) was rendered for the site; like the rest of the media it is not in git (MAINTAINING.md, **The site**).

## 1. Three rules

- **The picture leads; the words step back into the dark.** All text sits on the dark table outside the pools of light (the left 40 % of the home page), never on a bright prop; where needed a dark vignette carries it, never a coloured block or a card behind it.
- **Like a printed caption, not like an interface.** Hairlines, numbers, small type with tabular figures, serif headings. No rounded corners, no shadowed buttons, no gradient text, no glow.
- **The lamp colour touches one or two places.** `--lamp` is the pendant's 3200 K; at most two touches per screen: the current station's tick, "New", or one line on the first screen.

## 2. Colour

| Variable | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#0c0a09` | the page, text on light buttons; a little warmer than the picture's darkest |
| `--ink-2` | `#14110f` | panels (search, tips) |
| `--ink-3` | `#1a1714` | a picture's place before it loads |
| `--panel` / `--tip` | ink at 96 % / 92 % | the search panel and the corner player / small labels over pictures |
| `--paper` | `#efe7da` | headings, text, card names: the off-white of a print under a lamp, never pure white |
| `--paper-hi` | `#fff8ec` | a name brightening under the pointer |
| `--paper-2` | paper at 70 % | secondary text: the other language's name, the one-line description, film titles |
| `--paper-3` | paper at 52 % | metadata: numbers, lengths, ruler ticks |
| `--hair` / `--hair-2` | paper at 16 % / 30 % | hairlines, card outlines, link underlines |
| `--lamp` | `#e9b47c` | the current station, on the bench, new, the first screen's key line (Chinese) |
| `--lamp-2` | lamp at 55 % | card outline on hover |

- Contrast on `--ink`: `--paper` 16:1, `--paper-2` 8:1, `--paper-3` 4.8:1 (short metadata only), `--lamp` 10.5:1; all pass WCAG AA, and `check_site.py` checks them on every build. Text over a picture gets the same ground from the left-hand vignette (`.scrim`).
- One dark theme only: the picture is a night scene, so the page doesn't follow the system's light mode (`color-scheme: dark`).
- Colours are variables; a hard-coded colour in the CSS is a bug. The one exception is ink with an alpha inside a gradient, written `rgba(12,10,9,α)` (that is `--ink`).

## 3. Type (all SIL OFL, self-hosted, subset)

| Role | English | Chinese | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display: category names, style names, the first screen's heading, numbers, the `OPUSCAR` mark | **Source Serif 4** (variable, optical sizes 8–60, 400–600) | **Noto Serif SC 600** | the browser picks the optical size from the font size (no `font-variation-settings`); Latin letters and digits inside Chinese display lines take Source Serif 4 (it comes first in the stack), "AI" in the Chinese headline too |
| Italic second names, film titles | Source Serif 4 Italic (400) | — | on Chinese pages, English names are always Source Serif italic |
| Text and interface | **Source Sans 3** (400–600, italic 400) | **Noto Sans SC** (400–600) | on English pages (and the 404) the few Chinese words (the language switch, a search result's Chinese name) take the system's sans, so they never download the Chinese face |
| Metadata, times, numbers, keys (`--mono`) | Source Sans 3 with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` | as text | capitals with letter-spacing only for English metadata; figures line up in columns and don't jump while a time runs |

- The four variables stay: `--serif`, `--serif-sc`, `--sans`, `--mono` (`--mono` is now the text face with tabular figures; every rule that uses it sets `tabular-nums`).
- Subsets, made at every build from what the pages set: Latin (basic Latin and common punctuation) for Source Serif 4 (62 KB), its italic (40 KB), Source Sans 3 (17 KB) and its italic (10 KB), by `home.py`; Noto Serif SC only gets the display characters (the 15 category names, every style name, the first screen's heading, the notes), about 67 KB, and `pages.py` adds a second small subset for the characters its pages add, declared on Chinese pages only; **Noto Sans SC** is cut by `pages.py` once every page is written, to every Chinese character the site sets (2,124 today, 506 KB), one file for the whole site, declared for U+3000–303F, U+3400–9FFF, U+F900–FAFF, U+FF00–FFEF. Sources: all four families through `styleboard/fonts/fetch.py`, pinned to one google/fonts commit with their SHA-256; the OFL texts sit beside it and are copied into `<site>/fonts/`.
- What a page downloads in fonts (first visit, measured): English pages 117–194 KB; Chinese pages 663–712 KB, of which Noto Sans SC is 506 KB, fetched once and then cached for every page. If the Chinese text face grows past about 800 KB, split it (per page, or unicode-range slices) instead of shipping one file.
- Chinese display type must read easily: a text-grade Song face like Noto Serif SC, no calligraphic or decorative faces.
- `font-synthesis: none`: no fake bold or italic.

## 4. Grid and sizes

- **Two lines for the whole site**: everything set from the left hangs from one line, `--lx` (64u from the window's left edge; 16 px on phones), everything set from the right ends at `--rx` (the same from the right). The left line holds the mark, the first screen's words and its "Scroll" cue, every station's heading and cards, the ruler, the style page's label and reading part, the thank-you and 404 pages. The right column is `--rc` wide (258u) and ends on `--rx`: the top bar's search and language, and under them the contents on the first screen. The variables live on `html` (`app.css`), so a column that sets itself smaller keeps its edge. Captions placed on the picture itself (the notes, the finale's and OPUSCAR 98's caption under the projection) follow the picture, not the lines.
- **Frame coordinates**: the home page is laid out on a 1280 × 720 frame (`--u` = 1 px of that frame). In film mode `--u = clamp(0.8px, min(100vw/1280, 100vh/720), 2.4px)`: type scales with the picture.
- **One ladder of sizes.** Every size is a step, written `max(<floor>px, calc(var(--u) * <step>))`: it grows with the window and never falls under its floor, on phones too.

  | Step | At a 1280 × 720 window | Never below | Used for |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Label | 12u | 12 px | metadata, counts, times, ticks, keys, the ruler, the tag on pictures ("New"), fine print, the player's status and ruler numbers |
  | Small | 13u | 13 px | film titles under the cards, hints, the search field in the bar, the note's English line |
  | Interface | 14u | 14 px | buttons, names in three-across cards, the contents, tabs, the note's Chinese line |
  | Text | 16u | 16 px | running text in both languages (descriptions, the style page's about, fact sheet and install notes, OPUSCAR 98's descriptions, prompts and notes), names in two-across cards, inputs |
  | Lead | 17–18 px | 17 px | the first screen's lead, the style page's opening line |
  | Display | 22u and up | — | headings: OPUSCAR 98 22u (30u in the theatre), station names 36u English / 46u Chinese, the first screen 50u / 64u, style names 50u / 54u, numbers 60u |

  Chinese running text is never under 16 px and no label under 12 px, in either language.

  **One exception: short windows** (the film under 800 px tall, e.g. 1280 × 720, 1366 × 768, 1440 × 789). There every station's cards must fit without scrolling, so the station column's floors give way a step: labels (metadata, card lengths, film titles, tags, the ruler) 11 px, card names 13 px, the station's line 14 px; the thumbnails are cut a little lower (16:7.5) and the card lines set tighter, and `fit()` may set the column down to 64 % (78 % elsewhere) while it keeps its top and width. Windows 800 px tall and taller keep the ladder as it is.
- **The left column**: margin 64u, 446u wide (to 39.8 % of the frame), cards in three columns (two when a station has 4), 14u between columns, 12u between rows. The right 60 % is the picture's and the notes'.
- **Vertical rhythm** (each station): metadata → 12u → number + category name → 8u → second name → 10u → one line → 20u → every card (three columns) → the ruler at the bottom (18u from the edge).
- **It always fits**: when the column is too tall its u goes down (to 78 % at least, keeping 12u above the ruler): headings and gaps shrink, the text keeps its floor, the column keeps its top and width (`--u0`, the window's u); only below that do the cards scroll inside the column (fading at the bottom). On windows under 800 px tall the floors give way a step (the exception above), so the cards never scroll there either. OPUSCAR 98's caption, right of the ruler, fits down to the window's foot. Measured on 8 screens from 1024 × 600 to 2560 × 1440: everything fits within 40 %, no cut-off text, never smaller than 80 %, never scrolling.
- **Things placed in frame coordinates** (a note's dot, the finale's clickable projection, the finale's caption) follow the cover-fitted frame (CSS `--fs --fx --fy`, JS `coverBox()`), so they land on the same prop at any aspect ratio.
- **Phones and narrow windows** (< 1024 px): `--u = 1px`, 16 px side margins, running text at least 16 px and labels at least 12 px, touch targets at least 40 px; cards in two columns, all of them.

## 6. Motion (scrolling moves the camera)

- **Grammar**: scrolling down = the camera moving forward. On the way, about 1/70 of a screen per frame; at a station its stop frame holds for a stretch of scrolling (about 1.05 screens, from the long take's manifest) so the cards can be read; then it moves on.
- **Settling**: the picture follows the scroll position smoothly (time constant 70 ms); stopping close to a hold (within 0.26 screens) slides gently into it.
- **Going sharp**: when the camera has fully stopped on a station, its 2560 stop frame fades in over 0.42 s on top of the sequence; it fades back on leaving.
- **Cards appearing**: as a hold begins, the left column's blocks rise and fade in one after another (0.7 s, 40 ms apart); the note 0.45 s later.
- **Jumping** (contents, ruler, search, ← → keys, the browser's Back): when the video segments on the way are already loaded (usually the next station), the camera **fast-forwards** there in 0.5–1 s; otherwise a **cut through black**: 0.24 s to black, the scroll lands on the station, and once its 2560 stop frame is decoded it comes up in 0.6 s. Either way the scroll position matches the station and the history gets `#s05`, so Back returns to where you were.
- Easing `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`; hover 0.16–0.2 s, appearing 0.4–0.7 s.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`, Save-Data and phones: no long take, no animation, no hover clips (§9).

## 7. Words

- Say what it does and what it is: "Search 99 styles", "Scroll to walk the bench", "Watch the film". Not "explore", "discover", "unlock".
- Chinese: short, plain, full-width punctuation, film titles in 《》; a space before and after numbers and Latin words, as the rest of the library writes them.
- Counts come from the data (99 styles, 15 stations, N per station), never typed.
- The Chinese first-screen heading: 「挑一个风格，讲你的故事，片子交给 AI 拍。」 The commas at line ends are half-width (`halt`) and don't hang; "AI" uses Noto Serif SC's own Latin, so its weight, height and baseline match the Chinese. Avoid lower-case Latin words in Chinese display lines.
- Where the words come from: category lines from `gallery.py` (`CAT_DESC`); buttons, the first screen and the finale reuse the old site's words in `gallery.py` (`UI`); new words are in `home.py` and `pages.py` (`T`): "The Workbench / 电影工坊", "Station / 工位", "Next / 下一站", the notes. Same rules.

## 8. Two languages

- English at `/`, Chinese at `/zh/`, one language per page; the first visit follows the browser (`zh*` goes to Chinese); the switch is at the top right and the choice is kept in `localStorage` under `opuscar-lang`.
- **The one bilingual place** is the pair of category and style names, part of the layout (like a catalogue's bilingual labels):

| | Chinese pages | English pages |
|---|---|---|
| Station heading | Chinese name in Song at 46u, the English in Source Serif italic at 21u under it | English name in Source Serif at 36u (two lines when long), the Chinese in Song at 16u, 0.3em tracking, under it |
| Card | Chinese name + length; 《Chinese title》 (no English name in three columns; in two, the English italic name and the title share a line) | English name; italic English title + length |
| Note | a Chinese line + an English italic line | the English italic line only (a size larger) |
| Metadata | 「工位 05 / 15」, tabular digits | "STATION 05 / 15", spaced capitals with tabular digits |

## 9. Two layouts

- **Film mode** (`html.film`: at least 1024 wide and 560 high, motion allowed; smaller and the type gets too small): one full-screen canvas holds the long take; seventeen sections (the opening, 15 stations, the finale) lie over it, one lit at a time.
- **Stack mode** (`html.stack`: phones, reduced motion, Save-Data, no JS): the same HTML, each station a stop frame and its cards, top to bottom. On wide screens the stop frame is 16:9 full width with the cards over its dark left side (film mode's layout, standing still); on phones the stop frame is cropped 4:3 to the props and the cards follow it; the ruler stays at the bottom of the screen as the contents.

## 10. The pages under the bench

All pages share `app.css`'s variables, type and components; the top bar and the ruler are the same; each page is `--ink` and a picture of its own.

- **Style pages** (`/styles/<slug>/`):
  - **The first screen is the film itself**: the player fills it (leaving 40u for the next section's rule), at the film's own proportions, ink on either side on wide screens. The label steps back into the dark at the bottom left (the home page's station heading sizes, the same left edge `--gx`): "Station 02 / 15 · Painting & Illustration" (back to that station), No., the style name (Chinese pages: Song at 54u + English italic; English pages: Source Serif at 50u + Song), the film's title and length, the one-sentence about, "Watch the film" (the paper button) and "Make a film in this style".
  - **Grounds for the label and the top bar**: every poster is measured (`labelcheck.mjs` records where each line sits on the picture; `media.py --labels` takes the 95th-percentile brightness under it, WCAG: 3:1 for the name, 4.5:1 for the rest). A label that falls short gets a deeper pool under it (`.deep1–4`), a top bar that falls short a band at the top that holds dark and then fades (`.top1`); always the lightest level that passes. The lengths printed come from the film files (`media.py`'s meta.json), not from style.json's one-decimal copy.
  - **Lights down while it plays**: pressing play fades the label, the vignette and the top bar; moving the mouse brings back the controls and the top bar; pausing or the end brings back the label.
  - Below is the reading part, still on ink, same left edge; each section has a small "number · name · hairline · count" heading (tabular digits): 01 the demo film (its one-line story in large Source Serif, four stills under it that play the film from there); 02 make a film in this style (a printed sentence whose topic is a blank on a line, a copy button; the two ways to install, as tabs and command blocks); 03 STYLE.md (its sections listed like the home page's contents, each linking to that section on GitHub). On the right, the fact sheet (number, category, good for, film specs, folder, licence) and three document links, sticky while scrolling.
  - **Then the bench itself**: every style page runs on into the home page's long take, from its own station to OPUSCAR 98 and its credits (there is no "back to the bench" section, no previous / next and no footer). The bench is the same under every page of a language, so the pages don't carry it: `pages.py` writes it once per language, `media/bench-en.json` and `media/bench-zh.json` (the 15 stations and the finale drawn for a style page's depth, the ruler, the screening room, the take's track, the search index; about 125 KB, 30 KB gzipped), the page names it in `data-bench` with a content hash, and `app.js` fetches it, cached across pages, and puts it under the sheet; the stations before the page's own are hidden (their ticks on the ruler lead to the home page). Scrolling past the sheet hands over to the take: the camera waits on the station's stop frame while the sheet leaves, the station's words, note and ruler appear once the sheet has gone (`html.in-sheet` until then), and the top bar stays from there. Nothing of the take (segments, stop frames, card pictures) loads while the reader is on the film and its sheet; it starts a screen and a half before the bench, from that station's segment. **A card opens its film in place**: `app.js` fetches that style's page, swaps in its film and sheet (`page.js` binds them through `OCPage.init`), pushes its address, points the language switch, canonical and `hreflang` links at it, and runs the bench again from that style's station; it chains, and search picks a style the same way. Back brings the last film back with the reader where they left the bench (the swapped films are kept); Forward and a reload work, since the address is always a real style page. Phones: the same order with the stacked stations; the ruler waits until the bench is on screen. `check_site.py` checks that every style page names its station, slug and bench, has the film and sheet a swap takes, and that each bench holds every station, the finale, the ruler and the room, lists every style, and resolves every link from a style page.
- **OPUSCAR 98** (`/opuscar98/`, `pages.py` + `assets/o98.js`): the first screen is the finale's projector frame, fitted whole to the screen; **the player sits on the projection on the wall** (transparent at rest, showing only the viewfinder's play mark, so what you see on the wall is the projection itself). Playing grows it from the projection to the middle of the room (82 % wide, controls under the picture) while the lights go down (ink 93 %) and the caption fades; × at the top right leaves the screening. Reading down while it plays sends it to a small window at the bottom right (the search panel's ground), with a line on top: the film on screen (it jumps to that film in the book), "loop this film", ×; back at the top it grows again. - Before adding a component, look for one here; a new one follows the four rules: hairlines, square corners, paper text, one touch of lamp.

## 11. Don'ts

- No coloured card grounds, rounded corners, heavy shadows or frosted panels (the search panel is the one solid panel).
- No text on bright props; no other brand, trademark or logo on the picture.
- No second accent colour; the lamp colour is never a large ground.
- No full CJK font download; new display characters mean the subsets are made again (every build does).
- The interface never upstages the camera: on the way only the top bar, the ruler and "Next" stay; cards appear only when the camera has stopped.

# Facts on the plate and in the voice

*The Honeybee, Plate VII.* Every factual claim the plate prints or the voice says: where it comes from and the source's own words. The voice text is `lines.json` (also in `content.json`, with the plate labels); subtitles in `../engraving.srt`. Times are subtitle times. Checked on 2026-11-07.

## Sources

- [S1] M. Streinzer, A. Brockmann, N. Nagaraja, J. Spaethe, "Sex and Caste-Specific Variation in Compound Eye Morphology of Five Honeybee Species", *PLoS ONE* 8(2): e57702 (2013) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3584085/ — Table 1, *A. mellifera* worker, ommatidia per eye: "5,375±143 (4)" (mean ± s.d., n); drone "9,993±483 (4)"; queen "4,460±55 (2)". The paper measures "facet lens diameters", one lens per ommatidium.
- [S2] S. H. Eraghi et al., "Wing Coupling in Bees and Wasps: From the Underlying Science to Bioinspired Engineering", *Advanced Science* 8(16): 2004383 (2021) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8373159 — honeybee workers have "two pairs of wings, which are coupled to each other in flight via a complicated coupling mechanism on each side"; "a set of 20–22 minuscule hook-like structures, called as hooks, that are situated in a row on the leading edge" of the hind wing.
- [S3] V. Medved, Z. Y. Huang, A. Popadić, "Ubx promotes corbicular development in *Apis mellifera*", *Biology Letters* (2014) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3917345 — "Honeybee workers transport pollen on the tibial segment of the T3 leg", a concave region that defines the corbicula.
- [S4] UF/IFAS Extension, Putnam County, "Keep the Sweetness Coming: Celebrate National Honey Bee Day" (2024) — https://blogs.ifas.ufl.edu/putnamco/2024/08/16/keep-the-sweetness-coming-celebrate-national-honey-bee-day/ — "Despite their hard work, a single honey bee will produce only about 1/12th of a teaspoon of honey in its lifetime." The same figure is on the National Honey Board's trivia page, https://honey.com/newsroom/presskit/honey-trivia (not fetched: the site refused automated access).

IBTrACS best-track data

The files that REUSE.toml marks with LicenseRef-IBTrACS hold, or show,
records of the China Meteorological Administration (CMA) best track, as
distributed by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA),
National Centers for Environmental Information, in the International Best Track
Archive for Climate Stewardship (IBTrACS), version v04r01:
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-best-track-archive

These records are the CMA's data, not a work of the United States government,
so they are not marked as public domain here. NOAA describes IBTrACS as a
publicly available dataset whose usage policy follows the World Data Center for
Meteorology, with the World Meteorological Organization's Resolution 40 as the
guide for commercial use, and asks that it be cited:

  Knapp et al. (2010), Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society 91,
  363-376.
  Gahtan et al. (2024), IBTrACS version v04r01, NOAA National Centers for
  Environmental Information, https://doi.org/10.25921/82ty-9e16

Lemomo (LemoLab) grants no licence for these records. Use them on the terms of
their source. The demo's CREDITS and FACTS.md name the source and what was done
to the records.

# Silhouette Rhythm — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *Half a Beat* 《慢半拍》 (49.0 s) · `silhouette-rhythm.mp4` · source in [`demo/`](demo/)

A flock of flamingos moves on every beat, and one young bird is always half a beat late. No narration and no subtitles: the story is told by the poses, and the rhythm of the sound tells who is on the beat. The only words on screen are the end card.

## Story & structure

Logline: in a flock that moves on every beat, the youngest is always late; when the flock takes off at sunset it is left behind, falls on its first try, and finds the beat in the ripples the others left on the water.

Arc: the rule (five heads flag on the beat in front of the sun; a small head jumps up a whole beat late, then half a beat late, and bumps its neighbour) → the character (the march; it takes two steps for every one, misses the about-turn and walks into an adult) → the stakes (the flock lines up and takes off one bird every half beat; it is still dancing with its eyes shut) → the failure (its run is off the beat, feet and wings; it trips, a comic freeze, a record stop) → the low point (alone in the glitter, no beat at all) → the turn (a ripple passes under it on the beat, then another; a line of rings leads to the sun and lights one per beat) → the second try (it counts in with the opening's head flags, now on the beat, and runs one step into each ring) → the climax (it leaves the water in front of the sun and everything freezes: its wings and the flock's wings at the same point of the stroke) → the ending (it catches up with the line flying low across the last of the sun and falls into step; the title's last word lands half a beat late).

Native moves spent: *the beat in unison* as the hook; *the cascade* for the take-off; *the comic freeze* on the fall; *the beat written on the water* as the turn; *the freeze with the sky still moving* (with an orbit) as the signature shot; *the line across the sun* as the ending; *the black wipe* and a whip pan as transitions.

## Score structure

- 120 BPM, D minor through the dance and the run, a lift to F major at the freeze and in the chorus.
- **0–4**: kick and marimba only; the flags carry the rhythm. Adults are a low log drum on the beat, the young bird a bright woodblock off it. At 3.5 everything stops for half a beat except its 3.75 tick.
- **4–12**: full groove (marimba riff, plucked bass, kick, claps on 2 and 4, shaker, hats); tom fill into the about-turn; snaps on the line-up.
- **12–16.3**: double time for the take-off (eighth bass, sixteenth shaker, toms); each departing bird a marimba note higher than the last.
- **16.5–18**: a held low A, hats ticking, then nothing.
- **18–21.5**: the band keeps the beat it can't match (kick and bass on the beat, a frantic marimba slightly off the grid); its steps and wingbeats are audibly off. 21.2 a falling whistle, 21.5 a record stop.
- **21.5–28 (silence 1)**: only water, wind and distant calls; the splash at 22.38.
- **28–32**: the beat comes back one ring at a time: kick + conga + a rising kalimba / marimba note on eight beats; bass at 30, shaker at 31.
- **32–35.5**: the count-in (its woodblock now on the beat over a kick), snare roll; then one instrument per step (bass and kick, claps, marimba and hats).
- **35.5–36 (silence 2)**: half a beat with only its last step and wind.
- **36**: the hit (kick, crash, china, bass drum, wings), then a held F chord (strings, vibraphone, harp) under the freeze; a reversed cymbal into 39.
- **39–46**: chorus in F (F, C, B♭maj9, C) with a wing push on every beat; the title words use the characters' voices (HALF and A on the log drum, BEAT on the woodblock, late); 46.0 the final chord and one last woodblock half a beat after it; the tail rings to 49.
- Mix: music bus cut dead at the two silences with its reverb tail kept; character hits on their own bus so they survive the cuts; −14 LUFS, true peak −1.0 dB.

## Palette & props

- Figure #090307; sun #FFF6DC; the sky from gold #FFB347 and coral #FC4D2B (opening) through magenta #E0245C (alone) to violet #380A52 / #1A083D (after the freeze). The sun sinks from well above the horizon (shot 1) to a sliver (end card).
- **Adults**: 12 birds, small bodies, long legs, thin necks, smooth contours. Flight pose: neck forward, legs trailing, long narrow wings with separate primaries.
- **The young bird**: 0.72 × adult, round body with a downy scalloped outline (the only fuzzy contour in the film), shorter thicker neck, short straighter bill. It always stands on the left of the frame and at the back of the line.
- Water props: lingering "runway" rings (grow to ~0.55 m and fade over tens of seconds), fresh splash rings, crowns, falling drops. Reeds in the march, islands on the horizon.

## End card

On the dark water at the lower left, word by word on the beat, each with a small ring under it: **HALF** (42.5) **A** (43.0) **BEAT** (43.75, half a beat late), 慢半拍 (44.2), *Silhouette Rhythm · 剪影律动* (44.6), *LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5* (45.0); held to 49.0 while the line keeps flying. Anton 136 px, Noto Sans SC Black 64 px, Barlow Condensed 40 / 32 px, cream #FFF3E4. The "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5" sign-off belongs to this library's demo only; a user's film carries no LemoLab credit and no copy of this card.

## Build notes

```
demo/
  build.sh       one command: fonts (if missing) → events → score + foley → picture → master → readcheck → poster + style frame
  index.html     two canvases (WebGL sky/water/composite, 2D overlay for the whip blur and the end card), fonts
  film.js        render(t): camera, the cast and their schedules, runners, water light, every shot, freezes, motion blur, end card, EV (sound cues)
  shapes.js      parametric silhouettes: flamingo (stand/walk, young), flyer (flight/run/take-off), fluffEdge, palm, reeds, gull, boat
  sky.js         the fragment shader: sky ramps, clouds, sun, water, reflections, rays, rim, halo
  audio.py       score (sampled) + synthesised foley → out/mix.wav (49.000 s)
  make_fonts.py  subsets Anton, Barlow Condensed and Noto Sans SC to the end card's characters
  events.json    sound cues exported from window.EV
  CREDITS, fonts/OFL-*.txt, stills/styleframe.jpg
```

- `sh demo/build.sh` takes about a minute on Apple silicon (the picture renders 1470 frames in ~40 s with 2 workers; each frame 20–60 ms). In a fresh clone the fonts are not in git: the first run downloads Anton, Barlow Condensed and the Noto Sans SC variable font from github.com/google/fonts. The score needs `sh tools/fetch.sh instruments vcsl`, `vsco2ce`, `freepats` and `karoryfer`.
- `readcheck.mjs` passes: HALF A BEAT 6.5 s, 慢半拍 4.8 s, the style line 4.4 s (3.5 needed), the sign-off 4.0 s (2.9 needed).
- Everything depends on `t` only: walking and running feet are recomputed from the start of each shot, droplets are analytic, a freeze clamps subject time and the shader's noise time. Any second can be rendered on its own: `node core/render/still.mjs styles/silhouette-rhythm/demo 37`.
- Demo-specific pitfalls: the young bird standing among the adults is hidden inside their black bodies (it has to jump to be seen in shot 1); a black figure on dark water vanishes (shot 5 puts it in the glitter column); the line of rings in shot 6b would be lost in the glitter if the sun sat exactly on it, so the sun is a little off the line; the reeds use `ctx.filter = blur()`, which a renderer without canvas filters draws hard-edged without an error.

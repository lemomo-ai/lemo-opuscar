All strings are literals in `main_b.js` (search for the wording; the same strings are copied into the generated `main.js` and `index.html`). Shouts are in `voices/shouts.json`; subtitles `../game-show.srt` carry the shouts only. Times are film times from the tempo grid (`at(bar, beat)`, 150 BPM). The film was made in September 2026; checked on 2026-11-07.

Pages that refuse automated reading were read from the Internet Archive (the archived copy is linked beside the original), and posts on X through X's public embed endpoint.

## Readings and choices

- "One day early" compares Jodrell Bank's first publication on 4 February with the Soviet version "a day later"; the stamp lands on the Jodrell Bank print, not on the newspaper. Only RussianSpaceWeb gives the Soviet timing ("a day later"); the date 5 February on the official print's tag is derived from it.
- Sources give the distortion as a factor of 2 or 2.5. The film writes no factor and draws the Jodrell Bank print stretched twice as tall (the direction follows "peak-like").
- On the evening of 4 February the Moon had already risen some way (moonrise 13:33 UT on 3 February, Grahn); the low Moon at the start is a choice of picture.
- The photographs on paper are rendered from the film's own 3D Moon; no Luna 9 photograph is used.

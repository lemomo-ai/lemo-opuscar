Public domain

The files that REUSE.toml marks with LicenseRef-PublicDomain hold, or show,
third-party material that is in the public domain: works of the United States
federal government (Library of Congress catalogue records, NASA data, USGS and
NASA imagery, and the like), works whose copyright has expired (for example
films and prints published in the United States before 1931), works published
in the United States before 1978 without a copyright notice, works their authors
placed in the public domain, and map data that its makers placed in the public
domain (Natural Earth). Data files are kept as they were received, or cut,
converted or reprojected without adding anything original. No licence is needed
to use this material; its sources ask to be credited, as each demo's CREDITS
does. Works that are in the public domain in the United States may be protected
in other countries.

Where a file mixes such material with work of ours, or with material under
another licence, its licence expression in REUSE.toml names each, for example
"MIT AND LicenseRef-PublicDomain".
